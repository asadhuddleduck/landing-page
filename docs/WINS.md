# huddleduck.co.uk/wins

Public proof page (Asad, 12 November 2026). Every item lives in `src/lib/wins.ts`; the page renders only from that file.

## Rules
- Quotes are verbatim, original spelling kept. Cuts are marked with "...". Never use reworded quotes (Talk to Asad page) or Asad's second-hand notes of what a client said.
- Client-reported numbers say so. Views, impressions, followers and ticket registrations are never called sales or attendance.
- No individual client spend. No dates on client results (years only in the MR. ASAD section).
- Scores: show the full distribution from the Notion "NPS Responses" database, never an average or "10/10 average".
- OGS (NDA) and BellaMira never appear. The logo bar filters them with `exclude`.
- Left off on purpose: Phat Buns Uber Eats rep story, "hundreds of customers in 8 days", "£100 test", Liverpool "£13k under budget", Far East Kitchens quote (audio unchecked), Anna's Shark Aesthetics video (undecided), Ukor Victor's 2024 review (mentions AI).
- Copy: British English, no em or en dashes, no "AI", no hype words.
